Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding requirements, processing time, and document collection.

We accept any government-issued photo ID showing your full name and current address, including Philippine National ID (PhilID/ePhilID), Driver's License, Passport, UMID/SSS Card, Voter's Certificate, Postal ID, or Student ID (for minors/students).
Standard turnaround time is 24 to 48 working hours upon submission of complete requirements. Urgent requests for Certificate of Indigency for medical emergencies can be processed within the same business day.
Yes. An authorized representative must bring: (1) A signed Authorization Letter from the registered applicant, (2) The representative's original Valid ID, (3) A photocopy of the applicant's Valid ID, and (4) The tracking number.
